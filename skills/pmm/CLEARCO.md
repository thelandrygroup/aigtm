# ClearCo Overlay

Apply the top-level `CLEARCO.md` before using this skill.

For ClearCo product marketing work, tie artifacts to the buyer's talent lifecycle problem, not just product capability. Organize outputs by persona, use case, proof need, and lifecycle area: Hire, Succeed, Grow, or Platform.

Distinguish internal enablement from customer-facing language. Use only approved public or user-provided proof.

Do not invent adoption metrics, ROI, implementation timelines, AI claims, compliance guarantees, customer outcomes, or roadmap commitments. Keep internal-only material out of repo files.
