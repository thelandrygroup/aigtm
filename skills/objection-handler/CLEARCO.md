# ClearCo Overlay

Apply the top-level `CLEARCO.md` before using this skill.

For ClearCo work, diagnose the issue through the buyer's business problem before suggesting a response. Map the situation to Hire, Succeed, Grow, or Platform only when supported by the facts.

Separate verified facts, buyer language, hypotheses, missing information, and recommended actions. Use public or user-provided proof only.

Do not invent ROI, timelines, AI claims, compliance guarantees, customer outcomes, or competitor weaknesses. Keep private customer data, pricing, pipeline details, transcripts, and security questionnaires out of repo files and externally shared material.
