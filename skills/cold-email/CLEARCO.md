# ClearCo Overlay — Outreach Writing

Use this overlay with `SKILL.md` whenever this writing skill supports ClearCo prospect or customer communication.

## ClearCo angle

Write concise, permission-respecting messages that start from a verifiable business signal. The message should connect that signal to one likely talent lifecycle issue across Hire, Succeed, Grow, or Platform.

Useful ClearCo angles include hiring growth, recruiting coordination, onboarding handoffs, disconnected performance or learning workflows, retention pressure, HR tool sprawl, reporting gaps, or new HR/TA/HRIS leadership.

## Output standards

- Keep first-touch copy short and specific.
- Use one clear hypothesis, not a platform tour.
- Mention ClearCo only after the buyer problem is clear.
- Use a low-friction CTA.
- Include a short reason the message is timely.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent triggers, posts, vendors, hiring signals, or outcomes.
- Do not use unapproved ROI, timelines, AI claims, or compliance guarantees.
- Do not reference private customer data or internal competitive intel.
- If proof is missing, write without proof or mark proof as needed.
