# ClearCo Overlay — Sequence

Use this overlay with `SKILL.md` for ClearCo multi-touch communication plans.

## ClearCo angle

A sequence should progress from account signal to talent lifecycle pain to a relevant ClearCo conversation. Do not repeat the same pitch across touches. Each touch should add a new reason to care.

Recommended touch themes:

1. Account signal and likely pain.
2. Persona-specific business impact.
3. ClearCo lifecycle angle: Hire, Succeed, Grow, or Platform.
4. Relevant public proof or point of view, if approved.
5. Simple close-the-loop message.

## Persona mapping

- VP TA: hiring velocity, candidate experience, recruiter capacity.
- CHRO/CPO: lifecycle visibility, retention, manager effectiveness.
- HRIS/IT: integration, data quality, security, implementation risk.
- CFO/CEO: operating leverage, cost of fragmentation, measurable outcomes.

## Guardrails

- Use verified signals only.
- Keep each touch concise and respectful.
- Do not invent proof, ROI, timelines, or AI claims.
- Do not use private customer data in outbound copy.
