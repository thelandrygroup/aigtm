# ClearCo Overlay

Apply the top-level `CLEARCO.md` before using this skill.

For ClearCo work, build campaigns around a specific persona, trigger event, and talent lifecycle problem. Organize messaging around Hire, Succeed, Grow, or Platform when relevant.

Do not invent ROI, implementation timelines, AI claims, compliance guarantees, customer outcomes, or internal roadmap details. Keep private customer data, pricing, pipeline context, transcripts, and non-public competitive information out of repo files.
